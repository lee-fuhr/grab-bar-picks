How we choose our picks

We don’t run our own lab tests, but we do the next best thing: we dig into decades of owner reviews, talk to occupational therapists who specify grab bars for clients, and read published evaluations from respected consumer sources. What we look for first is mounting integrity, no bar is safe if the bracket or flange bends. Then we check the finish’s resistance to rust and pitting (common in cheap bars), and we favor brands with a long track record of durable bathroom hardware. We also monitor online forums and care facilities for reports of failures: broken flanges, stripped set screws, and finishes that peel in under a year. Every recommendation here has held up well in those real-world reports. Our top picks are models that consistently get high marks for ease of installation, a comfortable grip, and a secure feel when you really lean on them, not just when you test with a light touch.
